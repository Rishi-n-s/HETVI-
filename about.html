Our Story

How we became forever...

cake The Very Beginning

We first met in primary school back in the 6th standard. It was April 24, 2018—her birthday. We were only 11 years old back then, completely unaware that our paths were meant to cross for a lifetime.

hourglass_empty The Distance

We lost connection during the Covid era, and I ended up changing schools. But fate was just taking a pause.

smartphone Reconnecting

During 12th standard boards in 2024, we reconnected online on Instagram. At the time, she had no idea how badly I wanted her. In fact, she actually ignored me the first time we talked! But I kept trying, and we continued talking into 2025 during my first year of BCA. By the 2nd semester, we became really close friends, and I got hopelessly attached.

The Hackathon

In March 2026, we finally met again in person. Seeing her didn't feel like meeting a stranger; it felt like meeting the one made for me. At the hackathon, she was the first girl I ever hugged. And in that lift... I got my first kiss on my right cheek. A moment I will cherish forever.

diamond Forever Mine

After that, we got a chance to meet twice more—first at her home, then at mine. That’s when she found out I loved her. She didn't love me back yet 😢, but after all the hard work and tough times, I didn't give up. I got the opportunity to make her mine.

Now, she is not just my girlfriend, she is my fiancé. I love her so much, and the best part is... she started loving me back just as deeply.
